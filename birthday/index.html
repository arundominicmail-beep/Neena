You have brought so much happiness, comfort, love, and laughter into my life. Somehow, even the most ordinary days feel special when I get to share them with you. Thank you for being patient with me, for understanding me, for supporting me, and for loving me the way you do.

Happy Birthday, Neenu. Thank you for making my world brighter, kinder, and more beautiful simply by being in it.

I love you. ❤️

Yours, completely and always,

Your future husband
